Welcome

Welcome to the Data visualization course material for ITSE-1302 Computer Programming: Scientific Python at Austin Community College in Austin, 

eBook organization

The primary learning resource for this course is the eBook that you are now reading. This eBook is designed to provide everything that you need to know about visualization to be successful in this course.

The visualization section of the eBook consists of an index, seven lessons containing discussions and sample code for various visualization concepts, and a review lesson that is designed to help you prepare for the corresponding test.

In general, the order of topics presented in the lessons is keyed to the order of the topics appearing in the assignments. However, that may not be true in every case. You may find that you occasionally need to search through all of the lessons to find a discussion and sample code for a particular assignment requirement.

External online resources

Even though this eBook is designed to be the only necessary resource for this course, you may find that you can broaden your knowledge by visiting some external online resources. For example, the matplotlib gallery provides dozens of different data visualization examples along with the code required to produce those visualizations. You are encouraged to visit that website and take advantage of what you can learn by examining the images and the corresponding code.

Data visualization is a key competency for data science and analytics. One of the best and most comprehensive free online resources is Chapter 4. Visualization with Matplotlib. However, this is a free preview of an online Safari book and could be taken down at any time.
